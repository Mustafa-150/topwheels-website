COMMON QUESTIONS

Financing FAQs

What is a down payment?

A down payment is money paid upfront toward a vehicle purchase. It reduces the amount financed.

Does a longer loan term lower monthly payments?

A longer term often lowers the monthly payment, but may increase total interest paid over the life of the loan.

What is the difference between interest rate and APR?

The interest rate describes the cost of borrowing principal. APR generally includes the interest rate and certain financing charges, making it useful for comparing loan offers.

Does the calculator submit my information?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not submit its values to a dealership or lender.

Can I apply for financing on this website?

This website currently provides financing information only. It does not process loan applications or provide credit decisions.
