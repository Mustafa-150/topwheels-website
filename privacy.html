IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Understanding This Website

This website was developed as an IST 301 information organization project for TopWheels Int, Auto Sales LLC.

It currently uses demonstration vehicle records and browser-based interactive features. It is not configured to process credit applications or accept direct website form submissions.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when visitors use the TopWheels Int website.

The website provides vehicle information, search and filtering tools, an educational financing calculator, and a contact inquiry feature.

The explanations below describe the website as currently developed for this project. They should be reviewed if new services or data collection features are introduced.

2. Information You Provide

Visitors may choose to enter the following information into the Contact page:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Inquiry category
  • Message content
  • Vehicle information associated with an inquiry

Visitors should not include Social Security numbers, financial account details, payment card numbers, or other sensitive personal information in ordinary website inquiries.

3. How the Contact Form Works

The Contact page uses JavaScript to validate the information entered into the inquiry form.

After completing the form, visitors can review their information and choose to open an email application with a prepared message.

The website does not directly transmit the form to a server or maintain a customer inquiry database.

If a visitor sends the prepared email, the message is handled by the visitor's email provider and the recipient's email system. Those systems may retain the email according to their own practices.

4. Financing Calculator

The Financing page provides an educational monthly payment calculator.

Visitors may enter a vehicle price, down payment, annual interest rate, and loan term to calculate an estimated payment.

The calculation is performed locally in the visitor's browser. The calculator does not submit these values to a lender or dealership server.

The calculator is not a loan application, financing offer, or credit approval system.

5. Technical Information

The website uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to display content and provide interactive features.

Vehicle searches and filters run in the browser using demonstration inventory records.

Search terms may appear in the website address when visitors use the inventory search feature. Vehicle identifiers may also appear in page URLs.

Website hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Their practices depend on the hosting service used.

6. Third-Party Services

Some illustrative vehicle images on this project website are loaded from Unsplash image URLs.

When a browser requests an image from an external service, that service may receive technical request information.

The website may eventually be published through a static hosting provider such as GitHub Pages. Any hosting provider may process technical data as part of delivering the website.

Visitors who use email links may also interact with their own email applications and providers.

Before production use, this section should be updated to reflect all active third-party services and their privacy notices.

7. Data Security

This project website does not provide a customer database, online payment processing, or a credit application system.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee complete security.

Visitors should avoid entering sensitive financial information into the Contact form or sending it through ordinary email.

If the website later introduces direct form submissions, user accounts, or financial services, additional technical and organizational security measures will be required.

8. Your Choices

Visitors can browse the website without completing the Contact form.

Visitors may choose whether to open an email application and whether to send a prepared inquiry.

If an email has already been sent, requests about that correspondence may be directed to the recipient using the contact information below.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is designed to provide general automotive information and is not specifically directed toward children.

Visitors should not submit personal information about children through the inquiry form.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when website features, hosting arrangements, or information-handling practices change.

Updates should be reflected in the date shown at the top of this page.
